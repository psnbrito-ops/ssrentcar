About Us

Silvas & Santos Rent a Car is a family-owned company specializing in car rental, recognized for its professionalism, trustworthiness, and personalized service. We work daily to provide our customers with a comfortable, safe, and worry-free mobility experience.

We have a modern, diverse, and rigorously maintained fleet, with solutions tailored to different profiles and needs — from economical vehicles for everyday use to SUVs ideal for travel, leisure, and adventure.

Located in the heart of the tourist area of Santa Maria city, we offer excellent service, competitive prices, and close support at every stage of the rental process.

At Silvas & Santos Rent a Car, our commitment is to ensure comfort, quality, and trust so that you can enjoy every moment of your journey with complete peace of mind.
